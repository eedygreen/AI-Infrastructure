# Mission

Build: a semantic retrieval - The Transformer Backbone of a RAG System - internal assistant for employees that must answer employee questions using company documents, before it can generate an answer, it needs a way to find the most relevant documents for each question.

For: 
- Internal team (needs interpretable, summary, clarifications)
- Managers (Quick lookup on inventory)
- IT department (Troubleshooting and support).

Success means: the model achieves a high level of 95-99% accuracy, recall and acceptable precision in retrieval relevance.

Not in scope: the model will not be used for external usage, and will not replace the expertise of professionals. The model will also not be used for any commercial purposes.
