# Mission

Build: a neural network-based diabetes risk prediction model that can predict the likelihood of a patient having a diabetes disease based on their medical history and other relevant factors. The model should be trained on a dataset `data/diabetes_data.csv` and should be able to provide accurate predictions for new patients.

For: 
- Clinical team (needs interpretable, actionable results)
- Hospital administrators (cost-effectiveness concerns)
- IT department (requires deployment-ready model).

Success means: the model achieves a high level of 95-99% accuracy, recall and acceptable precision in predicting disease likelihood, and is able to provide actionable insights to healthcare providers and patients. The model should also be able to handle new patient data and provide predictions in a timely manner.

Not in scope: the model will not be used for diagnosis or treatment decisions, and will not replace the expertise of healthcare professionals. The model will also not be used for any commercial purposes.
