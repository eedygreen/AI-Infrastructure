🔥 Anti-Burnout Strategy

⚠️ Warning Signs:

  • Dreading study sessions
  • Procrastinating on easy tasks
  • Feeling overwhelmed constantly
  • Loss of interest in projects
  • Physical symptoms (headaches, fatigue)

✅ Recovery Actions:

  • Take 2-3 day full break (no guilt!)
  • Reduce to 10-12 hours/week temporarily
  • Switch to light learning (watch talks)
  • Work on fun side projects (no pressure)
  • Connect with community

🛡️ Prevention Tactics:

  • One full rest day/week (Saturday or Sunday)
  • No learning after 10 PM (protect sleep)
  • Celebrate small wins (finished a module? Treat yourself!)
  • Variety in learning (don't do the same thing 7 days straight)
  • Social learning (pair program, study groups)

📅 Emergency Rescheduling Protocol

Life happens. Use this when you miss sessions:

Missed 1 Day:

  • Add 30 min to next 4 days
  • No stress, back on track!

Missed 2-3 Days:

  • Reschedule full sessions to weekend
  • Cut 1 video course module if needed
  • Prioritize hands-on over theory

Missed 1 Week:

  • Pause and reassess
  • Extend month by 1-2 weeks
  • Don't compound guilt - just restart

Falling Behind >2 Weeks:

  • Review if 18 hours/week is realistic
  • Drop to 12-15 hours/week
  • Extend program to 8-9 months
  • Quality > Speed!
